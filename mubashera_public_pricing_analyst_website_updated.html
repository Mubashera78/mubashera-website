Value-Added Scenarios

How I Help Solve Pricing Problems

Illustrative professional scenarios—written without exposing confidential customer, company or pricing information.

01. Rate Mismatch

Problem: A system rate differs from the approved pricing reference.

Value I add: Validate → investigate → reconcile → document.

I compare the relevant rate inputs, trace the discrepancy and support the correct billing treatment.

02. Mileage & FSC

Problem: Mileage or fuel surcharge changes the final transportation cost.

Value I add: Verify the inputs before accepting the final price.

I review the applicable mileage and surcharge information so the pricing result is supported by available data.

03. Repeated Exceptions

Problem: Similar pricing exceptions keep appearing.

Value I add: Move from one-off fixes to pattern analysis.

I organize exception data, identify recurring patterns and provide a clearer basis for process improvement.

04. Billing Accuracy

Problem: Incorrect pricing creates billing discrepancies and rework.

Value I add: Strengthen the validation step.

By reconciling pricing information and documenting exceptions, I help reduce avoidable billing errors and improve confidence in the final rate.
